# Surface Brief — Servant Field Journey

- **Surface:** Servant dashboard → beneficiary list/detail → visit wizard.
- **Mode:** Operate. Fast, calm, low-cognitive-load field work.
- **Primary user:** A servant using a phone, often one-handed and sometimes under weak connectivity.
- **Primary job:** Understand today's priorities, reach the right beneficiary, and record a trustworthy visit with minimal hesitation.
- **Visual world:** «البيت الدافئ المتزن» — warm off-white paper-like surfaces, deep teal structure, restrained gold for meaningful emphasis, crisp Phosphor icons, Arabic-first type, and depth from spacing before shadow.
- **Hierarchy:** One dominant next action per screen; urgent context before historical context; identity and safety facts before secondary details.
- **Interaction:** 48px targets, persistent but non-obstructive visit action, compact bottom sheet, inline validation, explicit success/recovery, offline queue status, and visible keyboard focus.
- **Motion:** One restrained sheet transition; no decorative stagger, blur field, floating shapes, or continuous ambient animation.
- **Responsive contract:** 360–430px is authoritative; desktop expands composition without changing task order.
- **Accessibility:** Named controls, logical headings, focus trap/return for sheets, 4.5:1 body contrast, reduced-motion parity, RTL/LTR resilience, and no hidden dialog content in the accessibility tree.
- **Non-goals:** No route, Livewire event, authorization, database, or business-rule changes in the visual slice.
