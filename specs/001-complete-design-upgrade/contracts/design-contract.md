# Design Contract: The Balanced Warm Home

## THESIS

The interface makes the next act of care obvious. It refuses the generic KPI dashboard and the decorative church-themed app; operational context and the next safe action lead every screen.

## OWN-WORLD

Warm light grounds, Cairo as the workhorse face, deep teal for servant actions, calm blue for leadership actions, semantic colors only for state, quiet full borders, and elevation reserved for floating or active surfaces.

## STORY

Servants understand today, find a beneficiary, act, and recover from weak connectivity. Leaders understand what needs attention, inspect scoped evidence, and take the next authorized action.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Servant mobile opens with today and one dominant visit action, followed by urgent work and the shortest path to a beneficiary. Web App opens with role context, attention queue, and actions—not a wall of equal KPIs.

## FORM

Established-world extension in `Operate` mode. Compact task canvas, progressive disclosure, stacked mobile lists, split desktop detail, and functional state transitions.

## FINISH

unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
