# Product

## Register

product+brand

## Users

**Web App users** (service_leader, family_leader, super_admin): manage service groups, beneficiaries, teams, reports, and system configuration. They work primarily on desktop, switching between data management, team oversight, and operational planning.

**Servant panel users** (servant): field workers doing daily home visits, prayer follow-ups, and medical file management. They work on mobile devices during commutes or at homes, often in low-light conditions.

**Equally important.** Both surfaces must feel considered. Clarity and professionalism for all roles, with warmth that reflects the church mission.

## Product Purpose

A church ministry management system that streamlines the workflow of servants and leaders tracking beneficiaries, visits, prayer requests, medical files, and service groups. Success means servants spend less time on admin and more time on ministry. The system is Arabic-first, RTL-optimized, with PWA capabilities for offline field work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, Professional, Modern.

- Calm: no visual noise, no unnecessary urgency, reassuring whitespace
- Professional: precise, reliable, thoughtfully organized
- Modern: clean lines, current design language, web-native feel

Tone is warm-helpful rather than cold-efficient. The brand carries quiet dignity appropriate for church work without being overtly religious.

## Anti-references

- **Not enterprise/clinical**: no cold, sterile, hospital-like dashboards. Avoid slate/gray dominance. No aggressive blue gradients. No heavy data-dense grids that feel like ERP systems.
- **Not dark/heavy**: avoid dark themes as default, heavy shadows, aggressive colors. Keep the default experience light, airy, and calm.
- **Not traditional/ornate**: avoid overt religious iconography, old-fashioned aesthetics, or decorative borders.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over density.** Every pixel serves a purpose. Information is revealed progressively, not dumped. The app breathes.
2. **Warmth without decoration.** Warmth comes from color, spacing, typography, and micro-copy, not from decorative elements. No ornament for its own sake.
- **Mobile-first for servants, power-user ready for admins.** The servant surface is primary mobile; the web app is primary desktop. Both are excellent at their respective form factors.
4. **Quiet confidence.** The interface doesn't need to announce itself. Good typography, consistent spacing, and purposeful color do the work.
5. **Accessibility is non-negotiable.** WCAG AAA across both surfaces. High contrast, large touch targets, clear labels, reduced motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA target across all surfaces. High contrast ratios, large touch targets (min 48px), clear labels, reduced motion support, screen reader compatibility, focus indicators visible on all interactive elements. Arabic-first with English support. RTL-optimized layout and iconography that doesn't flip awkwardly.
